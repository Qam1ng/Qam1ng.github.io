About关于我

I am a first-year Ph.D. student at UCLA, advised by Prof. Lin F. Yang. I build agentic systems that let robots learn, reason, and act autonomously in human environments.

Previously I received my M.S.E. in Robotics from Johns Hopkins (LCSR, Intuitive Computing Lab with Prof. Chien-Ming Huang) and my B.S. in ECE from the University of Washington. I am also Cofounder & COO of KeploreAI.
